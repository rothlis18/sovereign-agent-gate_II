**4. Capital Autarky under Material Scarcity:**

Under conditions of strict material scarcity, capital autarky would be defined by the ability of a company to operate independently without external inputs. The structural boundaries of capital autarky would be determined by:

- **Critical Inputs**: The scarcity of critical inputs (e.g., rare earth metals, semiconductors) would limit the ability of companies to maintain operations.
- **Substitution Costs**: The cost of substituting scarce inputs with alternatives would impact the company's profitability and cash flow.
- **Operational Resilience**: The ability of the company to withstand disruptions in supply chains and maintain operations would define the boundaries of capital autarky.

In this scenario, companies with high exposure to scarce inputs and limited operational resilience would face significant challenges in maintaining capital autarky.

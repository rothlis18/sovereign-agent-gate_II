Up to 35% of the $1.7 trillion global private credit market is actively exposed to technology service sectors. This exposure creates a ticking time bomb, as corporate counterparties demand verifiable proof-of-work metrics over generic automated outputs. A sharp 10% structural down-round cascade across private equity tech valuations is imminent, triggered by the collapse of automated outputs.

**STRUCTURAL LOAN DEFAULTS**

Cloud-dependent companies will encounter inelastic infrastructure overhead as their recurring subscription revenues shrink by a third. This will trigger a cascade of structural loan defaults, as capital becomes increasingly scarce and illiquid. The precise physical mechanism of default is as follows:

1. Reduced subscription revenues lead to decreased cash flow.
2. Decreased cash flow exacerbates the liquidity crisis.
3. Liquidity crisis triggers a sharp increase in interest rates.
4. Increased interest rates render existing debt unsustainable.
5. Unsustainable debt triggers a wave of loan defaults.

1. Liquidity crisis: Cash flow collapse and decreased liquidity.
2. Interest rate shock: Sharp increase in interest rates.

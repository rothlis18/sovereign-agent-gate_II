**1. Downstream Cash Flow Collapse in Shadow Banking:**

The displacement of traditional enterprise software service models by next-generation autonomous AI agents and automated plugins will lead to a significant disruption in the shadow banking universe. This disruption is primarily due to the reliance of shadow banking on the stability and predictability of traditional enterprise software revenue streams. As AI and automation take over, the predictability diminishes, leading to increased risk and uncertainty.

**2. Impact on the $1.7 Trillion Global Private Credit Market:**

With up to 35% of the global private credit market exposed to technology service sectors, a demand for verifiable proof-of-work metrics over generic automated outputs will trigger a structural down-round cascade. Assuming a 10% reduction in private equity tech valuations, the immediate impact would be a $170 billion reduction in market value ($1.7 trillion * 10%).

**3. Mechanism of Structural Loan Defaults:**

Cloud-dependent companies facing inelastic infrastructure overhead while their subscription revenues shrink by a third will experience severe cash flow issues. The reduction in revenue by one-third implies a 33.33% decrease in cash inflows. If infrastructure costs remain constant, the cash flow gap will widen, leading to an inability to service debt obligations. This will result in structural loan defaults as companies fail to meet their financial commitments.

Under conditions of strict material scarcity, capital autarky becomes critical. Companies must rely on internal resources and efficiencies to sustain operations. The boundaries are defined by:

- Maximum sustainable cash flow: 0.6667X

### Forensic Financial Systems-Engineering Analysis

#### Downstream Cash Flow Collapse in Shadow Banking

1. **Displacement by AI and Automation:**
   - Autonomous AI agents and automated plugins are displacing traditional enterprise software service models.
   - This displacement affects the $1.7 trillion global private credit market, with 35% exposure to technology service sectors.

2. **Impact of Proof-of-Work Demands:**
   - Corporate counterparties demand verifiable proof-of-work metrics, leading to a 10% structural down-round cascade across private equity tech valuations.
   - This demand triggers a sharp decline in valuations, exacerbating cash flow issues.

3. **Structural Loan Defaults:**
   - Cloud-dependent companies face inelastic infrastructure overhead.
   - Recurring subscription revenues shrink by a third, leading to liquidity constraints.
   - The inability to cover fixed costs results in structural loan defaults.

#### Valuation Corrections

- **Initial Valuation Impact:**

  - A 10% down-round cascade reduces valuation to 0.9X.

- **Revenue Shrinkage:**

- **Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):**
  - DSCR = (Net Operating Income) / (Total Debt Service).
  - With reduced revenues, DSCR declines, increasing default risk.

#### Capital Autarky and Material Scarcity

1. **Capital Autarky:**
   - Companies must achieve self-sufficiency in capital.
   - Limited access to external funding due to valuation declines.

2. **Material Scarcity:**
   - Raw material scarcity increases production costs.
   - Companies face higher input costs without corresponding revenue increases.

3. **Structural Boundaries:**
   - Companies must optimize resource allocation.
   - Focus on core competencies and essential operations to maintain viability.

### Conclusion

- The displacement of traditional software models by AI and automation leads to significant valuation declines and cash flow issues.
- Demand for proof-of-work metrics exacerbates these issues, triggering structural loan defaults.
- Companies must navigate capital autarky and material scarcity to survive, focusing on efficiency and core operations.
